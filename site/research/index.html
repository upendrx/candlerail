Which crypto trading strategies survive an honest test?

Four studies, more than 110,000 backtests on real Binance candles with real fees and slippage. Every study states its question and its selection rule before looking at results, chooses on one period, and is then judged on data it never saw.

The short answer

Slow rules held up. Fast ones didn't.

Day-trading patterns lost to fees. Trend following on 4-hour candles, a weekly coin rotation and weekly engulfing candles with bitcoin's trend as a filter all made money on data they were never chosen on.

In plain words
  • These pages test trading strategies on real Binance prices from 2020 to 2026, paying real fees, and only trust results that repeat on data they weren't chosen on.
  • Fast day-trading patterns lost money after fees. Slower rules held: trend following on 4-hour candles, holding the strongest coins each week, and weekly engulfing candles traded only while bitcoin is rising.
  • Results are measured in R, the amount risked per trade. +1R means a trade won what it risked.

MHow every study is run

Testing hundreds of variants will always turn up some that look brilliant by chance. The method exists to tell those apart from rules that keep working.

1 · Ask

A question and a rule, fixed in advance

Each study writes down its strategies, settings, costs and the exact test a variant must pass before any result is seen.

2 · Search

Every combination, on every market

Setups are crossed with stops, targets, filters and timeframes, and every variant is backtested on every coin.

3 · Select

Choose on the first part only

A variant is selected if it clears the bar on the first two-thirds of the data (average result, trade count, most coins profitable).

4 · Judge

Judge on data it never saw

Selected variants are then judged on the last third, and the best of them again on other coins or earlier years.

The same rules for every backtest

  • Real data. Binance spot and futures candles, downloaded from Binance's public API and cached locally.
  • Real fills. Signals are read at a candle's close and filled at the next candle's open. When a candle touches both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed to fill first.
  • Real costs. Taker fees and slippage on every fill: 0.05% plus 0.01% on futures, 0.1% plus 0.02% on spot, and double costs as a stress test.
  • R multiples. Results are measured in R, the amount risked on a trade. +1R means a trade won what it risked; −1R means the stop was hit.
  • Dead coins count. Studies 3 and 4 include coins that collapsed or were delisted (LUNA, FTT, SRM and others), so holding a dying coin counts as a loss.

!What these results are not

Backtests, not promises

Every number here is historical. Markets change, and several results were weaker in recent years than earlier ones. Nothing on these pages is financial advice.

  • Multiple testing. With thousands of variants some survive by luck; that is why survivors are re-tested on coins or years they never saw, and why a single survivor (Study 1) is treated as noise.
  • Survivorship. Studies 1 and 2 use coins that still trade today, which flatters every strategy, buying and holding included. Studies 3 and 4 add coins that died.
  • Lumpy returns. The slow rules make most of their money in a few strong months and sit flat or in cash for long stretches.
  • Execution. Fills at the next open are realistic for liquid coins at small size; large orders, thin coins and exchange outages are not modelled. Futures funding is not included in the directional studies.

RReproduce any result

Every study is a JSON file in research/, and every figure on these pages is drawn from the recorded results in research/results/. Candlerail is a single open-source binary that downloads the data and runs the whole grid locally.

cargo build --release
./target/release/candlerail study research/swing-trend.json --digest swing-trend.json
./target/release/candlerail serve        # the app, with its Research tab and variant explorer
