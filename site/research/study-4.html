Study 4 · Weekly swings, market opens, early entries

Weekly patterns, entered on the daily chart

Do weekly candle patterns work across many coins, especially with bitcoin's trend as a filter? Are intraday trades better around the London and New York opens? Does entering early on the hourly chart help? And would any of it survive trading through one real account?

78,016 backtests43 coins, including dead onesMay 2020 to Sep 202624 unseen coins
Verdict: yes for weekly, no for intraday

Weekly engulfing with BTC's trend: about +1R a trade, on seen and unseen coins.

The session filter and hourly entries didn't help. The weekly rule held up in a one-account replay, more weakly on new coins since 2024.

In plain words
  • We looked for candle patterns on the weekly chart and entered on the daily chart, on 43 coins including ones that collapsed.
  • The best rule: after a bullish engulfing week, buy when a day closes green above the previous day's high, and only while bitcoin is above its 50-day average. About +1R a trade, also on 24 coins it never saw.
  • Through one account risking 0.5% a trade it made about +43% a year with a largest fall of 21%, but most months are flat and a few big months made most of the gain.
  • Timing trades to the London and New York opens, and entering early on hourly candles, didn't help.

1The case

Study 2 found that slower rules pay; Study 1 found that candle patterns on fast charts don't. A weekly candle summarises a whole week of trading. If candle patterns mean anything, they should mean more there.

Three ideas are tested together: weekly patterns entered on the daily chart, with bitcoin's trend as a filter for every coin; intraday setups only in the hours after the London and New York opens, when volume arrives; and daily patterns entered early on hourly candles, to get a tighter stop. The best rule is then replayed through one account, with every signal on every coin competing for the same money.

Hypotheses

(1) Weekly patterns, filtered by BTC's trend, average at least +0.1R a trade on at least 20 of 43 coins, before and after the split. (2) Limiting intraday setups to the market opens improves them. (3) Hourly entries improve daily patterns.

2What was tested

Six weekly setups, each traded long and, in some variants, short (the mirror image).

Where the pattern must appear

Here the "place" is the bigger picture: the pattern must be on the weekly chart, the entry waits for the daily chart to confirm it, and bitcoin must be in an uptrend. Each of these filters raised the average result; see what each one did →

Entry

At the next week's open, on a daily close above the previous day's high, or on a pullback to mid-week then a green day.

BTC filter

None, BTC above its 50-day average, or BTC above its 20-week average.

Stop and exit

Beyond the pattern week or 2 ATR; a 2R or 3R target, or hold up to four weeks.

Markets

43 Binance coins including LUNA, FTT, SRM and other coins that collapsed or were delisted.

3How it was tested

4Weekly patterns: 369 selected, 127 survived

6 setups × 3 entries × 3 BTC filters × 2 stops × 3 exits × 2 sides × 2 costs, on 43 coins: 49,536 backtests.

Long-only medians after the split, spot costs: with BTC above its 50-day average +0.21R, without a filter +0.09R, with BTC above its 20-week average +0.02R; a daily confirmation +0.28R, entering at the week's open +0.05R. Holding up to four weeks (+0.30R) beat 2R and 3R targets. Engulfing and marubozu (strong-bodied) weeks held up; hammers and three soldiers turned negative.

5The pick

The rule

Every day after the 00:00 UTC close, for each coin: if last week (Monday to Sunday) was a bullish engulfing week, today's daily candle closed green and above yesterday's high, and BTC's daily close is above its 50-day average, buy at the next open. Stop 2 ATR (14-day) below the entry. Sell after 28 days if the stop hasn't been hit.

6Intraday around the market opens

Opening-range breakouts and false breaks after the first 30 minutes of the London and New York opens, and Study 1's reversal setups limited to 1, 2 or 4 hours after the open or allowed any time, on ten coins at 5 and 15 minutes over a year: 14,080 backtests.

7Daily patterns, hourly entries

Four daily setups (engulfing, hammer or shooting star, morning or evening star, inside-day breakout) traded on hourly candles or, for comparison, on daily candles, on ten coins over six years: 14,400 backtests.

8One account, real costs

The backtests above give each coin its own share of the account. A real account has one pool of money: signals on different coins arrive together, positions overlap, and some signals must be skipped. The replay trades every signal on all 43 coins through one account, in date order, with a limit on open positions.

9Conclusions

  • Weekly engulfing candles, confirmed on the daily chart and filtered by BTC's trend, were the strongest pattern result in these studies, and held on coins the rule never saw.
  • BTC's trend is the most useful filter for other coins: most coins fall when BTC does.
  • The market opens didn't help intraday setups, and entering early on the hourly chart made results worse.
  • Expect long flat stretches. The account is in cash most of the time and earns in bursts.

Limitations

The pick is one of 576 combinations tested under each cost level. The 24 unseen coins are young, and all trade in the same market as the original 43, so they are not independent of it. Returns depend on a few strong months, and recent results on new coins were weaker.
